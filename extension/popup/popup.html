Web Job Scraper

Fit markers

On LinkedIn, jobs whose title fits your Job Finder titles are highlighted with a "Fit" tag, and an open job shows its whole description with a Job Fit score.

Hidden companies

Companies you hid with the X on a LinkedIn card. Their jobs stay out of the list. Companies blocked in Job Finder are hidden too.
